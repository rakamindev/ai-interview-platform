## What this proves and what it does not
CONFIRMED (local, 6 Nov):
- The app boots in production mode with eager loading (zeitwerk:check "All is good!").
- A production-mode Puma server starts, /api/v1/health returns 200 behind an HTTPS proxy header,
  authentication is still enforced (401 without a token), Sidekiq reaches the local Redis (PONG).
UNVERIFIED (not proof of deployment readiness):
- Real deployment secrets (a random dummy SECRET_KEY_BASE was used) and the production database
  (DB_NAME pointed at the local development database).
- Kubernetes behaviour: api/k8s/deployment.yaml probes call plain-HTTP /health with no
  X-Forwarded-Proto, while production forces SSL (FORCE_SSL defaults to true) and redirects with 301.
  Whether probes pass depends on cluster config not in this repo. Escalated, not changed.
- Production Redis/Sidekiq under the real cluster, clustered Puma (R3), and a real WebSocket audio call.
